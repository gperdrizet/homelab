    the-educator¶

    Home theater PC, mostly used for streaming. Also runs the Minecraft server.

    Hardware¶

    CPU: Intel Xeon E3-1290 (8 threads) RAM: 16 GB Storage: 240 GB SSD (system), 2× 3.6 TB HDD (ark, big_itch) OS: Ubuntu 22.04 LTS

    Network¶

    • LAN: 10.1.10.204
    • Not enrolled in the tailnet.

    Services¶

    Service Notes
    papermc.service Minecraft server — see service page
    NFS client fstab entry still points at decommissioned arkk (10.1.10.202) — broken, no nofail; see network/nfs.md
